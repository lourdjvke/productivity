02 — A TINY TASTEYou can copy this.
It really is this small.

One attribute.
One clean destination.

Mark the links LEO should handle. Everything else behaves like a normal link.

HTML / 01
index.htmlHTML
<!-- This link is handled by LEO -->
<a leo-pid="/contact">Get in touch</a>

<!-- This is still just your page -->
<main>
  <h1>A nice, ordinary page.</h1>
</main>
NOTEUse leo-pid on links you want LEO to route. Your normal anchors stay normal.
